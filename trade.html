Repair & Reproduction

Replacement parts for hardware that is no longer manufactured.

When the original manufacturer has gone out of business, discontinued a line, or never sold a replacement in the first place, the part still has to come from somewhere. We reverse-engineer it from an existing sample and produce as many as you need -- one, or several hundred.

Common for obsolete cabinet and door hardware, panel latches, window and railing components, and architectural elements in older buildings where matching the existing profile matters more than catalog availability.

Before You Inquire

What's the minimum quantity?

One. Single replacements and prototypes are normal work for us, not exceptions.

Do you need the physical part?

For simple geometry, dimensioned photographs with a scale reference next to the part are often enough. Complex profiles and anything that has to fit an existing mechanism need the sample in hand.

Do I get my sample back?

Yes -- it ships back with the finished order. Reverse-engineering is non-destructive.

Can you match the original finish?

Finish specification is part of the quote. Tell us what the surrounding hardware looks like and we'll match to it.

What materials and processes?

Polyamide and other durable polymers via SLS/FDM, plus CNC milling and laser engraving -- assembled and finished in-house in Michigan.

What about load-bearing or safety-rated parts?

Tell us the application up front. If a part carries structural load or needs a code rating we can't certify, we'll say so rather than quote it.
