Commercial Hardware Reconstruction

Large commercial development required reproduction of 240 custom latches on existing electrical panels after the original manufacturer ceased operations. VOLDT reverse-engineered the hardware from physical samples, developed parametric models to accommodate dimensional variation across door types, and produced all components to finish specification.
