Bespoke Project

Fully custom design + fabrication, scoped and quoted.

For projects that require a new geometry, a new system, or a one-off object with production-level rigor. We combine parametric design, reverse engineering, and small-batch fabrication to deliver parts and objects that are install-ready.

Services

  • —Parametric Systems — custom algorithmic design for product families and architectural components
  • —Reverse Engineering — part reconstruction and reproduction for discontinued/damaged components
  • —Digital Fabrication — SLS/FDM printing, CNC milling, laser engraving; assembled and finished as needed

Case Study

Commercial Hardware Reconstruction

Large commercial development required reproduction of 240 custom latches on existing electrical panels after original manufacturer ceased operations. Components featured complex geometry with no available technical documentation.

Voldt reverse-engineered the hardware from physical samples, developed parametric models to accommodate dimensional variation across door types, and produced all components matching finish specification.

Scope

240 components across 6 size variations

Timeline

8 weeks from sample receipt to delivery

Outcome

Full replacement hardware matching original specifications
