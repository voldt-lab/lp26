About the Design

A blend of technical innovation and artistic expression, our Detroit light series explores geometric details and textures drawing from Art Deco architecture - specifically, the Fisher Building's chandeliers located in downtown Detroit. When the light is on, crisp vertical lines appear and contrast with a warm internal glow.

The lamp offers direct and ambient light perfect for bedrooms, kitchens & living spaces.
