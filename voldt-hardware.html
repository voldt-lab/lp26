Award-winning configurable architectural hardware system. Algorithmic variation creates coherent families of cabinet and door pulls with precise control over size, finish, and mounting detail.

Concept

VOLDT Hardware employs parametric design to generate a coherent family of pulls and handles. Each variation maintains the algorithmic DNA of the system while offering precise control over dimensional and material properties.

The configurator presents finite predefined options rather than unlimited customization, ensuring every output maintains design integrity and manufacturability.
