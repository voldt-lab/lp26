About the Collection

PolyFrame Collection designs are made to order. Please allow 3-5 weeks for fulfillment.

Designed and crafted in Michigan. Made of solid black walnut with 3D-printed nylon and brass details.

VOLDT's PolyFrame series is an algorithmic furniture system inspired by architectural forms.

Through this series, we are reimagining furniture design, fabrication and assembly processes by fusing handmade techniques with contemporary technologies. Transition details celebrate this by juxtaposing smooth walnut edges with sharp 3D-printed forms.

Materials are expertly selected to balance performance, sustainability and aesthetics. Recycled nylon and TPU joints are connected to durable walnut beams, while recycled PETG diffuses light through captivating textures.

3D-printed nodes act as assembly guides for irregular angles, resulting in a network that plays with our perception of stability.

Materials

  • Solid American black walnut, hand-finished
  • Recycled nylon 3D-printed nodes
  • Recycled TPU joints
  • Recycled PETG light diffusers
  • Brass hardware and detailing
