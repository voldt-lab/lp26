Design Approach

Detroit Lights employ parametric surface patterning to control light diffusion and shadow casting. The algorithmic texture creates visual interest while serving functional requirements for ambient and task lighting.

Series A features a dense cellular texture for soft diffused light. Series B employs a linear pattern for directional illumination with architectural shadow play.

MATERIALS & FINISH

Shade is made from recycled semi-translucent PETG, recycled polyactic acid (plant-based polymer)

SPECIFICATIONS

  • For use with LED bulbs only, 60W max
  • Includes one (1) 3500k LED bulb
  • UL Listed File No. E365544
  • Polarized 2 Blade plug
  • Interior Use Only
  • Cord Length: 12'-0" with on/off switch (Table Lamps)
  • Mounting: Ceiling mounted with Voldt Wall Bracket included (Pendants)

LEAD TIME

All designs are made to order - please allow up to two weeks for shipping.
